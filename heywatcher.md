# Hey Watcher

Mosaic is a privacy-first iPhone app for FinanceHER that answers: “What changed on my credit report?”

The user imports a credit-report PDF, reviews neutral source-linked changes, classifies what needs attention, and organizes editable recovery packets, evidence, and deadlines. The original PDF stays on-device; only redacted structured facts may reach cloud AI.

Gemini provides concise summaries and editable drafts. Auth0 secures sessions. Synthetic data demonstrates the full scan → classify → packet → tracking flow.

Mosaic never diagnoses abuse, fraud, identity theft, or coercion; makes legal conclusions; stores raw PDFs in AI memory; or submits disputes automatically. The user remains in control.

The experience should stay calm and discreet: clean white surfaces, the Twilight Purple Dreams palette, native SF Symbols, and Apple Liquid Glass navigation and actions.
